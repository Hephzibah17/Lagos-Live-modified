How to Use the Lagos Cowry Card

The Cowry Card is the unified electronic contactless transit card used across all Lagos BRT buses, Blue Line Rail, Red Line Rail, and Lagferry boats.

Where to Get One
Any BRT or Rail Station Terminal (₦500 initial card cost)
Mobile App Top-Up
Instant top-up via Cowry App or bank USSD (*329#)
Tap In / Tap Out
Always tap out at destination turnstiles to ensure standard fare debit

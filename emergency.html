Lagos Emergency Services & SOS

Direct access to verified 112/767 toll-free dispatch, police, fire, ambulance, and trauma centers.

Immediate Danger or Life Threat

Dialing 112 or 767 connects immediately to the Lagos State Emergency Management Agency (LASEMA) Central Command Control. Calls are toll-free from any mobile network in Nigeria.

CALL 112 (TOLL-FREE) CALL 767
